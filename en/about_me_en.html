Jakub Klokan

My story began on September 11, 2008, when I was born late at night in Most, Czech Republic.
My childhood was quite ordinary. I  attended 4th Elementary School in Most for the first stage of my education.
After fifth grade, I transferred to the eight-year program at Podkrušnohorské Gymnázium in Most.
I first encountered badminton in first grade, and I immediately knew it was what I wanted to pursue.

Badminton Career

From a young age, I loved movement, speed, strength, explosiveness, and precision — coincidentally, the very qualities every badminton player needs.
I started playing badminton at the age of 6 at the Super Stars Most where I still train under coaches Lukáš Kroc and Veronika Brožkovcová.
At first, I played just for fun, without visions or goals. Over time, as I began winning tournaments and receiving awards, my coaches started telling me I had talent and a real chance for a successful career. That’s when a goal — and a dream — was born in my mind: to reach the Olympic stage.

School

Over the years, I attended two schools and studied three different programs. In 2016, I started first grade at Václav Talich Elementary School in Most, where I spent five years before transferring to the eight-year program at Podkrušnohorské Gymnázium Most.
Those four years at gymnasium were challenging, very different from elementary school. But they taught me how to study effectively, work independently, and meet deadlines.
After completing compulsory education, I changed my program to what I study today: Sports Gymnasium at Podkrušnohorské Gymnázium Most.
After high school, I plan to continue my studies at university. I haven’t fully decided on a field yet, but it will likely be related to badminton. We’ll see where life takes me.

Education and Courses

Alongside school, I strive to learn beyond the classroom. I have completed several courses and training programs that expanded my knowledge and skills.
A big thank you goes to the Super Stars Most team, who supported my participation in these programs.

  • Completion of elementary education with annual honors (Most 2024)
  • BWF Shuttle Time Youth Leadership (Bahrain 2024)
  • Mental Health Course (Bahrain 2024)
  • WAoS Athlete Certificate (Online - Most 2024)

Social Media

In summer 2022, I decided to share my journey and inspire others with what I do. I started posting videos and updates from tournaments, training sessions, international trips, and a glimpse into my personal life.
Social media has become a secondary hobby — time is limited, but I  strive for the best results. I would be very happy if you start following me on my social channels. I am most active on Instagramu. Thank you!

My name is Jakub Klokan, and I am a top-level badminton player from the Czech Republic. I compete in both national and international tournaments, from which I have brought home numerous medals.
I am also a student at the Podkrušnohorské Sports Grammar School in Most.
