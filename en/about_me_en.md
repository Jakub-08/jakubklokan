<h2 class="h2-about-me">Badminton career</h2>

<div class="section-divider-1 about-me">
<span></span>
</div>


<p>

Since childhood, I&nbsp;have always loved movement, speed, strength,
explosiveness and&nbsp;precision. Coincidentally, these are exactly the
qualities every badminton player needs.<br />

I&nbsp;started playing badminton at&nbsp;the age of 6 in the badminton club
<a href="https://www.superstars-most.net/" target="_blank">
Super Stars Most</a>,
where I&nbsp;still play today under the guidance of coach Lukáš Kroc.<br />

At&nbsp;first, I&nbsp;played only for fun, without any vision or goals.
Over time, as I&nbsp;started winning tournaments and receiving awards,
my&nbsp;coaches began telling me that I&nbsp;had talent and a&nbsp;chance
for a&nbsp;successful career.<br />

That is when a&nbsp;goal was born in my&nbsp;mind. A&nbsp;dream.<br />

To&nbsp;reach the Olympic rings.

</p>

<h2 class="h2-about-me">Educational journey</h2>

<div class="section-divider-1 about-me">
<span></span>
</div>


<p>

Over time, I&nbsp;have attended two different schools and studied three
different fields.<br />

In 2016, I&nbsp;started first grade at
<a href="https://www.gymmost.cz/" target="_blank">
Václav Talich Elementary School in Most</a>.
I&nbsp;spent 5&nbsp;years there before transferring to the eight-year
grammar school at
<a href="https://www.gymmost.cz/" target="_blank">
Podkrušnohorské Gymnasium Most</a>.<br />

I&nbsp;studied there for 4&nbsp;years. They were quite challenging years,
with a&nbsp;completely different environment compared to elementary school.
However, they taught me how to study effectively, work independently and
meet deadlines.<br />

After completing compulsory education, I&nbsp;changed my&nbsp;field of study,
and I&nbsp;am still continuing there today. I&nbsp;study at the sports
gymnasium at
<a href="https://www.gymmost.cz/" target="_blank">
Podkrušnohorské Gymnasium Most</a>.<br />

After finishing high school, I&nbsp;would like to continue at university.
I&nbsp;do not have a&nbsp;clear idea about the exact field yet, but it will
probably be connected with badminton. We will see where life takes me.

</p>
