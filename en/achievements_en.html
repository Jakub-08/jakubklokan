Estonian Youth International

The last international tournament of the 2022 season was a great success. I earned podium finishes in singles (2nd place) and doubles (3rd place), losing only in the semifinals to the eventual winners.

Adria Youth International

An international tournament I participated in several times, almost always winning medals. In 2021, I earned 2nd place in doubles, and in 2022, I expanded my medal collection with singles and doubles victories.

Czech Republic U15 Championship 2021

My first Czech National Championship, winning two bronze medals: in  doubles with Kryštof Klíma and mixed doubles with Terka Grznárová.

Grand Prix B U13

In 2020, I won my first national tournament in Karlovy Vary at the Grand Prix B U13.

19th / 20th Trobitz Youth International

My first international tournament: a singles victory and 2nd place in doubles. The following year, I achieved a podium sweep: 1st in mixed doubles, 2nd in singles, 3rd in doubles.

My name is Jakub Klokan, and I am a top-level badminton player from the Czech Republic. I compete in both national and international tournaments, from which I have brought home numerous medals.
I am also a student at the Podkrušnohorské Sports Grammar School in Most.
